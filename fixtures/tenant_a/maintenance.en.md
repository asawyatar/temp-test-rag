# Maintenance at Qamar Bay

## How to raise a request

Open the resident app, choose "Service requests", pick a category and describe
the problem. Add a photo if you can: it helps the technician bring the right
parts. You will get a notification each time the status of the request changes.

## Response times

Emergency requests, such as a water leak, a power failure or a jammed lift, are
attended within 2 hours at any time of day. Standard requests are handled within
3 working days. The maintenance team works Sunday to Thursday, 08:00 to 17:00.

## What is free and what is charged

Repairs to shared areas and to the building structure are free. Repairs inside
a unit are charged to the resident. An air-conditioning service visit costs
150 SAR per unit. The technician gives a price before starting any charged work,
and the charge is added to your next invoice.

## Access to your unit

A technician enters a unit only when an adult resident is present, unless the
resident has given written permission in the app. In an emergency that puts
other units at risk, the management office may enter with security staff.
