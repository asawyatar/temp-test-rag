# Booking Facilities at Qamar Bay

## Padel court

The padel court costs 40 SAR per hour. Bookings open 7 days in advance and a
single booking can last up to 90 minutes. Cancel at least 4 hours before the
start time for a full refund; later cancellations are not refunded.

## Community hall

The community hall can be booked for private events at 500 SAR per evening. A
refundable deposit of 1,000 SAR is held until the hall is inspected after the
event. The hall holds up to 80 guests and must be vacated by 23:00.

## Barbecue area

The barbecue area is free to use and is booked in three-hour slots. Each unit
may hold one upcoming barbecue booking at a time. Please leave the grills clean.

## How to book

All bookings are made in the resident app under "Facilities". Payment is taken
when the booking is confirmed.
