# Qamar Bay Community Rules

These rules apply to all residents, family members and guests of Qamar Bay.

## Swimming pool

The swimming pool is open every day from 06:00 to 22:00. On Mondays the pool
opens late, at 10:00, because of weekly cleaning. Children under 12 must be
accompanied by an adult at all times. Glass containers are not allowed in the
pool area. A lifeguard is on duty from 09:00 to 18:00.

## Gym

The gym is open 24 hours a day. Residents aged 16 and above may use the gym.
Access is by the resident app: scan the QR code at the gym door. Please wipe
down equipment after use and return weights to the rack.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 22:00 to 07:00 every day. During quiet hours, residents
should avoid loud music, drilling and moving furniture. Renovation work is only
allowed between 09:00 and 17:00, Sunday to Thursday.

## Pets

Each unit may keep up to two pets. Every pet must be registered with the
community management office within 14 days of arrival. Dogs must be kept on a
lead in all shared areas. Pets are not allowed in the pool area or the gym.

## Parking

Each unit is allocated two parking spaces. Visitor parking is located next to
Gate 2 and is free for up to six hours. Vehicles left in visitor parking for
longer than 48 hours may be towed at the owner's expense.
