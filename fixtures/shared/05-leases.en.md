# Leases

### Q: Where can I see my leases in the Atar app?
**A:** Open **My Leases** (from your account, or from the Lease Details card on a unit). The list shows every lease linked to your account.

### Q: What does each lease card show?
**A:** The community name, the unit name, the remaining days of the lease, the total rent (excluding VAT) and the next payment date.

### Q: How do I filter my leases?
**A:** Use the filter chips at the top: **All**, **New**, **Active**, **Expired** and **Terminated**.

### Q: Can I search for a lease?
**A:** Yes. Search by lease number. If nothing matches, the app shows "No Matches Found".

### Q: What lease details can I see?
**A:** The lease record shows:
- Community name, and the unit's type and category.
- Lease type and rental type.
- Lease number and lease creation date.
- Property handover date.
- Lease start date, lease end date and contract end date.
- Rent-free period, if any.
- **Rental Transaction Schedule**: the planned rent payments.
- **Annual Rent Increase** (escalation), if any.
- Yearly rent excluding VAT.
- The **Rental Contract** document, which you can open.
- A link back to the unit's details.

### Q: How do I download my rental contract?
**A:** Open the lease and tap **Rental Contract**. The contract opens in your phone's browser or PDF viewer, where you can save or share it.

### Q: What do the lease statuses mean?
**A:** **New**: the lease has been created but has not started yet. **Active**: the lease is currently in force. **Expired**: the lease end date has passed. **Terminated**: the lease was ended early.

### Q: My lease list is empty. Why?
**A:** "No Lease Found" means no lease is linked to your account. Owners who live in their own unit usually have no lease. Tenants should contact property management if their lease is missing.

### Q: Can I renew or terminate a lease from the app?
**A:** No. The app shows lease information only. Renewal, termination and changes are handled by your property-management company.
