# Home Screen and Navigation

### Q: What is on the Atar home screen for residents?
**A:** The home screen greets you by name (Good Morning / Good Afternoon / Good Evening) and shows:
- The latest community **announcements**.
- **Quick actions**: Request a Service, Add a Visitor, View Announcements, Send a Suggestion, and Book a Facility.
- **My Units**: your units, which you can swipe between.
- **My Dues** summary: Upcoming and Overdue amounts.
- Your **upcoming facility booking**.
- **Benefits** (offers for residents).

### Q: What quick actions are on the home screen?
**A:** Request a Service, Add a Visitor, View Announcements, Send a Suggestion, and Book a Facility. Each one opens that flow directly.

### Q: What is the guided tour?
**A:** After your first sign-in the app offers a 20-second tour ("Welcome home"). It highlights announcements, requesting a service, adding a visitor, sending a suggestion, booking a facility, offers, your units and your dues. Tap **Start Tour** or **Maybe Later**. You can replay it any time from Account Settings → **App Tutorial**.

### Q: How do I move around the app?
**A:** Use the bottom tab bar. Owners and tenants have Home, My Dues, Services, Visitors and Benefits. Service professionals have Home, Jobs and Visitors.

### Q: Where do I find Units, Facilities, Directory and the other sections?
**A:** Open **My Account** (your profile). The More menu lists Units, Announcements, Additional Users, Directory, Facilities and Suggestions, along with Edit Profile, Account Settings and My Digital ID.

### Q: What does a service professional see on the home screen?
**A:** "Welcome back" followed by their name, and **Today's Tasks** split into three tabs: Common Area Services, Unit Services and Visitor Requests. If nothing is scheduled, the screen says "No tasks today".

### Q: What are pop-ups that appear when I open the app?
**A:** Property management may be waiting on you for something. The app shows these one at a time when you sign in: a request to **rate a completed service**, a **new survey** to fill in, or (for owners) a **handover agreement** to sign.

### Q: Does the home screen work offline?
**A:** No. If there is no internet connection, the app shows "No internet connection." Reconnect and pull down to refresh, or tap Retry.
