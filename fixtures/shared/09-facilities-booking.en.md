# Facilities Booking

### Q: What are facilities in the Atar app?
**A:** Facilities are the community's shared amenities, such as the gym, swimming pool, cinema, sports courts or event halls. You can see them and book them from the app.

### Q: Where do I find facilities?
**A:** Open My Account → **Facilities**, or tap **Book a Facility** on the home screen. The page has two parts: **My Facilities** (the facilities available to you) and **My Bookings**. Tap **View all** to see all facilities.

### Q: What is the difference between "Bookable" and "Open Access" facilities?
**A:** A **Bookable** facility must be reserved for a time slot before use. An **Open Access** facility can be used freely during its working hours without a booking.

### Q: How do I know whether a facility is open?
**A:** Each facility shows an **Open Now** or **Closed** badge, based on its working hours. Facilities that work all day are always open.

### Q: What information does a facility page show?
**A:** Photos, a description, the community name, working days, working time, age limit, gender (All, Male or Female), a contact number, the maximum booking duration and the booking limits.

### Q: How do I book a facility?
**A:**
1. Open the facility and tap **Book Now**.
2. Choose your **Unit**.
3. Pick a **Date**.
4. Choose a **Duration**.
5. Pick one of the **Available Times** for that duration.
6. Agree to the Terms and Conditions and confirm.

### Q: What durations can I book?
**A:** Durations go in 30-minute steps (30 minutes, 1 hour, 1.5 hours, and so on) up to the facility's maximum booking duration.

### Q: Why can't I book a facility that has age or gender limits?
**A:** To comply with legal and safety regulations, some facilities restrict booking by age and/or gender. If your profile is missing your date of birth or gender, the app asks you to update your profile first. Tap **Go to profile**, fill in the missing details, then book again.

### Q: What is the booking limit?
**A:** A facility can limit how many times each resident can book it per month. If you've reached it, the app shows "Reached Booking Limit: Sorry, you have reached your monthly booking limit for this facility."

### Q: There are no available times on my chosen date. What do I do?
**A:** The slot is fully booked or the facility is closed that day. Choose another date or a shorter duration.

### Q: What are the booking statuses?
**A:**
- **Awaiting Approval**: management still has to approve your booking.
- **Approved**: your booking is confirmed.
- **Rejected**: management declined it. The rejection reason is shown on the booking.
- **Cancelled**: the booking was cancelled. The cancellation reason is shown.

### Q: How do I see my bookings?
**A:** Under **My Bookings**, the **Upcoming** tab lists future bookings and the **Past** tab lists earlier ones. Each booking shows its number, date, start and end time and status. Your next upcoming booking also appears on the home screen.

### Q: How do I cancel a facility booking?
**A:** Open an upcoming booking that is Awaiting Approval or Approved and tap **Cancel Booking**. Choose a reason ("Booking time is not correct", "I'm not available", or Other) and confirm. Past bookings can't be cancelled.

### Q: Why don't I see any facilities?
**A:** "No Facility Found" means your community has no facility available to you yet. Facilities appear once management makes at least one available.
