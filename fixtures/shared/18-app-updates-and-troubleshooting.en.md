# App Updates and Troubleshooting

### Q: The app says "Amazing new features… Please update". What should I do?
**A:** Your app version is no longer supported. Tap **Update Now** to open the App Store or Google Play and install the latest version. You can't continue with the old version.

### Q: The app says "Update ready". What does that mean?
**A:** A small improvement has been downloaded in the background. Tap **Restart Now** to use it right away, or **Later**, and it is applied the next time you open the app.

### Q: The app shows "Updating the app" with a progress bar. What is happening?
**A:** An important update is being installed. Keep the app open. It only takes a moment.

### Q: What does "No internet connection" mean?
**A:** Your phone is offline. Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and try again.

### Q: What does "Our servers are having a moment" mean?
**A:** The Atar servers had a temporary problem. Wait a little and try again.

### Q: The app says "Something went wrong". What should I do?
**A:** Tap **Retry** or go back to the home screen and try again later. If it keeps happening, sign out and sign in again.

### Q: How do I refresh a screen?
**A:** Pull down on the list to refresh it.

### Q: The app can't use my camera or photos. How do I fix it?
**A:** Allow camera and photo access for the Atar app in your phone's settings. The app needs them for request photos, profile pictures and scanning visitor QR codes.

### Q: Switching language restarted the app. Is that normal?
**A:** Yes. Changing between Arabic and English restarts the app automatically so the whole layout can switch direction.
