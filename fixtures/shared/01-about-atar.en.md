# About the Atar App

### Q: What is the Atar app?
**A:** Atar is a mobile app for people who live in, own, or work in residential communities managed on the Atar Cloud property-management platform (goatar.com). Residents use it to see their units, follow their dues, request home services, invite visitors, book community facilities, read community announcements, and use resident benefits. Service professionals use it to manage the jobs and visitor check-ins assigned to them.

### Q: Who can use the Atar app?
**A:** The app supports three account types:
- **Owner**: a person who owns one or more units in the community.
- **Tenant**: a person who leases a unit.
- **Service Professional**: a technician or staff member who carries out service jobs and handles visitor check-ins.

Accounts with any other role (for example an administrator or manager) cannot use the mobile app yet. They see a "Coming soon to mobile" screen and are asked to use the web portal instead.

### Q: What can an owner or tenant do in the Atar app?
**A:** Owners and tenants can:
- View their units, unit documents, floor plans and location.
- View their leases and lease payment schedule.
- Track dues (invoices, payments, overdue and upcoming amounts) and download invoices and receipts.
- Request home services (plumbing, cleaning, AC and more), approve quotations, and rate the service.
- Invite visitors and share a QR check-in pass with them.
- Book community facilities such as the gym, pool or cinema.
- Read community news and events, and RSVP to events.
- Use exclusive benefits and offers from partners.
- Send suggestions to the community management team.
- Add family members or authorized occupants as additional users.
- Browse the community directory of contacts.
- Answer surveys sent by property management.
- Show their digital ID (QR code).

### Q: What can a service professional do in the Atar app?
**A:** A service professional sees today's tasks on the home screen and can accept or decline jobs, start a job with a site-assessment photo, raise a quotation, complete the job with a completion photo, collect payment, and confirm completion with the customer's 4-digit confirmation code. They can also scan visitor QR passes and check visitors in and out.

### Q: Which languages does the Atar app support?
**A:** Arabic and English. Arabic is the default language, and the whole app is laid out right-to-left when Arabic is selected. You can switch the language from Account Settings. The app restarts automatically to finish switching.

### Q: Does the Atar app support dark mode?
**A:** Yes. In Account Settings → Theme you can choose Light Mode, Dark Mode, or System (follow the phone's setting).

### Q: Which devices does the Atar app run on?
**A:** The Atar app is available for iPhone (iOS) and Android phones.

### Q: What are the main sections of the Atar app for residents?
**A:** The bottom tab bar has five tabs for owners and tenants: **Home**, **My Dues**, **Services**, **Visitors**, and **Benefits**. Further sections (Units, Announcements, Additional Users, Directory, Facilities, Suggestions, profile and settings) are reached from the **My Account** menu.

### Q: What are the main sections of the Atar app for service professionals?
**A:** Service professionals have three tabs: **Home** (today's tasks), **Jobs**, and **Visitors**.

### Q: Is my data in the Atar app tied to a specific community?
**A:** Yes. Each account belongs to a business (the property-management company) identified by its business username. Everything you see (units, dues, facilities, announcements, directory) comes from the communities that business manages for you.

### Q: Where can I read the Atar privacy policy and terms of use?
**A:** Open My Account → Account Settings and tap **Privacy Policy** or **Terms of Use**.

### Q: Where is the Atar website?
**A:** The Atar website is https://www.goatar.com.
