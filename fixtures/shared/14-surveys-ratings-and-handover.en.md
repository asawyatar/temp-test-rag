# Surveys, Rating Prompts and Handover Agreement

### Q: Why does a pop-up appear when I open the app?
**A:** Property management may be waiting on you. When you sign in, the app shows any pending items one at a time:
- **Rate Your Experience**: rate a service request that was completed.
- **New Survey**: a survey from the property-management team.
- **Handover Agreement**: an owner's agreement to sign when receiving a unit.

The next pop-up appears when you finish or dismiss the current one.

### Q: How do surveys work?
**A:** The property-management team can send you a survey. You'll see a **New Survey** pop-up describing it. Tap **Fill Survey Now** to answer or **Later** to dismiss it. Tapping a survey notification opens the form directly.

### Q: What kinds of questions can a survey have?
**A:** Four kinds: a short written answer, a single choice, a multiple choice, and a date. Required questions show "Please answer this question" if left empty. Tap **Send** to submit.

### Q: Can I answer a survey more than once?
**A:** No. A survey can be answered only once. If you open it again, the app says "You have already answered this survey".

### Q: A survey says "This survey has closed". Why?
**A:** The survey's deadline has passed and it no longer accepts answers.

### Q: What is the handover agreement?
**A:** When an owner receives (takes handover of) their unit, property management can ask them to sign a **Handover Agreement** in the app. It shows the date, community, unit and buyer information. The owner enters their ID number and taps **Agree** to sign.

### Q: What are the rules for the ID number on the handover agreement?
**A:** It is required, may not contain special characters, and can be at most 15 characters.

### Q: Do I have to rate a service right away?
**A:** No. You can dismiss the pop-up and rate later from the request's details page, or from a rating reminder notification. If you have already rated, the app shows your existing rating.
