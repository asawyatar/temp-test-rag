# Dues and Payments

### Q: What is "My Dues" in the Atar app?
**A:** My Dues is where owners and tenants keep up with what they owe: invoices, payments and outstanding balances related to their units and leases. It is a tab in the bottom bar and is also summarized on the home screen.

### Q: Who can see My Dues?
**A:** Owners and tenants. Service professionals do not have a dues section.

### Q: What do "Upcoming" and "Overdue" mean?
**A:** **Upcoming** is the amount that will be due soon. **Overdue** is the amount whose due date has already passed and needs paying now.

### Q: How do I filter my transactions?
**A:** Use the filters at the top of My Dues: **All**, **Overdue**, **Outstanding** (not yet paid, not yet late) and **Paid**. You can also search your transactions.

### Q: What does "Due in 5 days" or "Overdue for 3 days" mean on a transaction?
**A:** It counts days from today to the due date. "Due in N days" means the due date is still ahead, "Due today" means it is today, and "Overdue for N days" means the due date passed N days ago. "Paid" means the transaction is settled. An unpaid transaction whose due date has passed is shown as overdue even if it was originally listed as outstanding.

### Q: What details can I see for a transaction?
**A:** Tap a transaction to open Transaction Details:
- **Total Amount** and **Remaining** balance.
- **Invoice Details**: Transaction ID, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Due Date and Lease Number.
- **Payment History**: payments recorded against the invoice.
- **Description & Attachments**.
- A **Download Invoice** button.

### Q: How do I download an invoice?
**A:** Open the transaction and tap **Download Invoice**. The invoice opens as a PDF. On iPhone it opens in Safari, and on Android it is saved through your browser to Downloads.

### Q: How do I get a payment receipt?
**A:** In Transaction Details, open a payment under **Payment History**. The Payment Details screen shows service fees (including VAT), payment status, invoice number, payment method, payment time and description. Tap **Download Receipt** to get the receipt PDF.

### Q: Do amounts include VAT?
**A:** Payment details show service fees including VAT, with the VAT rate stated (Saudi VAT is 15%).

### Q: My Dues is empty. Why?
**A:** "No Transactions Found" means no invoices or payments have been issued for your account yet. They appear as soon as property management issues them.

### Q: A transaction says "This transaction is no longer available". Why?
**A:** Property management may have removed it. Go back and pick another transaction.
