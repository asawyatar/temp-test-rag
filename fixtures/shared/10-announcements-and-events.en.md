# Announcements and Events

### Q: What are announcements in the Atar app?
**A:** Announcements are messages from your community management: important updates, notices, community news and upcoming events. The latest ones appear at the top of the home screen.

### Q: Where do I find all announcements?
**A:** Tap **View Announcements** on the home screen or open My Account → **Announcements**. The page shows **Latest News**, **Latest Events** and **History**, with **News** and **Events** tabs.

### Q: What is the difference between News and Events?
**A:** **News** is information (for example a maintenance notice or a policy update). An **Event** is a gathering with a start and end time and often a location, which residents can RSVP to.

### Q: How do I RSVP to an event?
**A:** Open the event and choose **Attending** or **Not Attending**. When you attend, the app shows "You're Attending". You can change your answer until the RSVP deadline.

### Q: What happens when an event is full?
**A:** If the event has a seat limit, the page shows the seats available. When it's full, the app says "This event has reached its maximum capacity. RSVP is no longer available."

### Q: What happens after the RSVP deadline?
**A:** The app shows "RSVP Closed" and no longer accepts responses.

### Q: Can I see who else is attending?
**A:** Yes. Tap **Who's Attending** on the event to see the residents who RSVP'd.

### Q: How do I add an event to my calendar?
**A:** Open the event and tap **Add To Calendar**. The app needs calendar access. If it's off, turn it on in your phone's settings.

### Q: How do I find the event location?
**A:** If the event has a location, tap **View On Map**.

### Q: An announcement says "This announcement has been deleted". Why?
**A:** The community manager removed it, and it is no longer available to view.
