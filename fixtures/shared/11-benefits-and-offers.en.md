# Benefits and Offers

### Q: What are Benefits in the Atar app?
**A:** Benefits are exclusive deals and property perks for residents, offered by partner businesses (for example discounts at restaurants, gyms or shops). They are in the **Benefits** tab.

### Q: Who can see Benefits?
**A:** Owners and tenants. Service professionals don't have the Benefits tab.

### Q: How do I browse benefits?
**A:** Open the **Benefits** tab. Filter by category, or choose **All**. Each card shows how many days are left before the offer ends.

### Q: How do I redeem an offer?
**A:** Tap the offer to see its start and end time and **How to redeem the offer**, then tap **Redeem Offer**. When it succeeds, the button changes to **Redeemed**.

### Q: Is Atar responsible for the offers?
**A:** No. Benefits shown in the app are provided by third parties. Atar is not responsible for, and does not guarantee, the availability of product quantities or their prices.

### Q: Why is the Benefits tab empty?
**A:** "No benefits Available Right Now" means no partner offers are active at the moment. New benefits appear when they become available.
