# Service Professional Jobs

### Q: What is a service professional in Atar?
**A:** A service professional is a technician or staff member who carries out service requests for residents (unit services) and maintenance of shared spaces (common-area services), and who checks visitors in and out.

### Q: What is the difference between Unit Service and Common Area jobs?
**A:** A **Unit Service** job is requested by a resident for their own unit. It includes customer details, an assessment fee, a possible quotation, payment collection and a confirmation code. A **Common Area** job is maintenance of the community's shared spaces. It has community details instead of a customer and a shorter flow.

### Q: Where do I see my jobs?
**A:** The **Jobs** tab lists your jobs with tabs for **Unit Service** and **Common Area** and the total count. **History** shows past jobs. Today's work also appears on the Home screen under **Today's Tasks**.

### Q: How do I filter or sort jobs?
**A:** Tap the filter button. Sort by Newest first, Oldest first, Latest scheduled or Earliest scheduled, and filter by status and service. Tap **Apply**, or **Clear** to reset.

### Q: What actions can I take on a job?
**A:** Depending on its status: **Accept**, **Decline**, **Start**, **Raise Quotation**, **Complete Job**, or **Cancel**.

### Q: What reasons can I give to cancel or decline a job?
**A:** Incorrect request information, Duplicate request, Request not applicable to common area service, Issue already resolved, Canceled based on management decision, Out of service policy, or Other. A written reason is required.

### Q: How do I start a job?
**A:** Tap **Start**. On the **Site Assessment** screen, upload an **Initial Assessment Photo** of the issue (JPEG, PNG, WebP, SVG, HEIC or HEIF, up to 15 MB), add an optional description, and tap **Proceed to Quote**. For a common-area job, tap **Start Request**. Clear, well-lit photos speed up approval.

### Q: How do I raise a quotation?
**A:** Tap **Raise Quotation**. Review the service fee, then add **Spare Parts** and **Additional Fees** as title-and-amount lines. The app shows the grand total including 15% VAT. Tap **Raise Quotation** to send it to the customer, who must accept it before the extra work starts.

### Q: How do I complete a unit-service job?
**A:**
1. Tap **Complete Job** and upload a **Job Completion Photo** that clearly shows the finished work and any new parts, with no tools left in the shot. Add a description of any adjustments.
2. **Payment Collection**: check the total collection amount and payment breakdown, then tap **Confirm & Collect**. This sends a 4-digit confirmation code to the customer's registered mobile number.
3. **Confirmation Code**: ask the customer for the code, enter it, and tap **Complete Job**.

### Q: Can I comment on a job?
**A:** Yes. Open the job and tap **Comments** to share updates or discuss the request with the customer and management.

### Q: What does the work progress on a job show?
**A:** The steps the job has gone through: Started, Quote Raised, Quote Accepted or Quote Rejected, Completed, or Cancelled. You also see initial-assessment and completion photos, the ticket ID, scheduled time, description and attachments.

### Q: How do I call the customer?
**A:** On Job Details, under **Customer Details**, tap **Call customer**.
