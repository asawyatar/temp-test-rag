# Profile, Digital ID and Settings

### Q: How do I edit my profile?
**A:** Open My Account → **Edit Profile**. You can change your photo, first name, last name, email, phone number, national ID, nationality, gender and date of birth. Tap **Save**.

### Q: What are the rules for profile fields?
**A:**
- First and last name are required and may use Arabic or Latin letters, underscore and space only.
- Email must be a valid address.
- National ID must be 10 digits starting with 1, 2 or 3.
- Phone number is required, with a country code.

### Q: How do I change my profile photo?
**A:** On Edit Profile tap **Change photo**, then **Take Photo** or **Choose from Library**. The photo must be under 30 MB. The app needs camera or photo access.

### Q: Why should I fill in my gender and date of birth?
**A:** Some facilities have age or gender limits, and you can't book them until your profile includes these details.

### Q: What is My Digital ID?
**A:** A personal QR code that identifies you in your community. It shows your name, photo and "Member since" date. For owners and tenants the QR carries their unit; for other accounts it carries their phone number. Tap **Share QR** to send it.

### Q: What does My Account show?
**A:** Your name, photo and role (Owner, Tenant or Service Professional), plus links to My Digital ID, Edit Profile, Account Settings and the More menu (Units, Announcements, Additional Users, Directory, Facilities and Suggestions).

### Q: How do I change the app language?
**A:** Account Settings → **Language**. Choose العربية or English. The app restarts automatically to finish switching.

### Q: How do I switch between light and dark mode?
**A:** Account Settings → **Theme**. Choose Light Mode, Dark Mode or System.

### Q: How do I replay the app tutorial?
**A:** Account Settings → **App Tutorial**.

### Q: How do I check my app version?
**A:** Account Settings → **App Version**.

### Q: How do I delete my Atar account?
**A:** Account Settings → **Delete Account**. To confirm, type the word **DELETE** in the field and confirm. This action cannot be undone, and all your data is permanently removed.

### Q: How do I log out?
**A:** Account Settings → **Log Out**.
