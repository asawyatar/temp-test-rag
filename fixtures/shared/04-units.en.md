# Units

### Q: What is a "unit" in the Atar app?
**A:** A unit is a property you own or lease inside a community managed on Atar, such as an apartment, villa, office or shop. Each unit belongs to a community and may belong to a building. It has a category (for example Residential) and a type (for example Apartment).

### Q: How do I see my units?
**A:** Your units appear in the **My Units** section of the home screen, where you can swipe between them. For the full list, open My Account → **Units**. Each card shows the unit's photo, name, community, building and city/district.

### Q: Why don't I see any units?
**A:** The list only shows units you own or lease. If it says "No Units Yet", your account has no unit linked to it yet. Contact your property management to link your unit.

### Q: Can I search my units?
**A:** Yes. The units list has a search field, and the search runs across all your units, not only the ones loaded on screen.

### Q: What details can I see for a unit?
**A:** Tap a unit to open Unit Details. Depending on what property management has filled in, you can see:
- Unit photos (tap to view full screen).
- The community, building, category and type.
- **About this Unit**: a description, with Read more / Show Less.
- **Unit Specifications**: for example Year Built, Net Unit Area (sqm) / GLA, rooms and areas.
- **Features & Amenities**: for example parking, pool or gym.
- **Floor Plan**: swipeable floor-plan images.
- **Documents**: the files attached to the unit.
- **Lease Details**: lease type, lease end date and status (Active or Expired).
- **Unit's Location**: a map, with Share Location and Get Directions.

Sections with no data are hidden, not shown empty.

### Q: How do I open my unit's documents?
**A:** On Unit Details, tap **Documents (n)**. A sheet lists the unit's files and you can search them by name. Tapping a document opens it with your phone (PDFs and images open in the viewer or browser).

### Q: Why can't a tenant see the handover contract?
**A:** The unit's handover contract is an owner document. Owners see it in the unit's documents; tenants do not.

### Q: How do I get directions to my unit?
**A:** On Unit Details, scroll to **Unit's Location** and tap **Get Directions**. On iPhone you can choose Apple Maps or Google Maps. **Share Location** sends the unit's address through your phone's share sheet.

### Q: Why is there no map on my unit?
**A:** The map only appears when the unit has a complete location saved by property management. If the location is missing, the location section is hidden.

### Q: Can I edit my unit's details or list it for sale or rent from the app?
**A:** No. Unit information is managed by your property-management company. The app shows it read-only for owners and tenants.

### Q: How do I see the lease for my unit?
**A:** On Unit Details, the **Lease Details** card shows the lease summary. Tap its lease number to open the full lease record. See "Leases" for details.

### Q: What does the unit's lease status mean?
**A:** **Active** means the lease is currently valid. **Expired** means its end date has passed.
