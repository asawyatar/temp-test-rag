# Notifications

### Q: What notifications does the Atar app send?
**A:** Updates related to your property, requests, bookings and payments. For example: service-request status changes, new comments on a request, rating reminders, new surveys, visitor updates, facility-booking decisions, announcements and new offers.

### Q: Where do I see my notifications?
**A:** Tap the bell icon to open **Notifications**. The list shows all notifications, newest first, with how long ago each arrived (for example "5min Ago" or "2h Ago").

### Q: What happens when I tap a notification?
**A:** The app opens the related item, such as the service request, survey or offer. A rating reminder opens the rating sheet for that request.

### Q: How do I mark all notifications as read?
**A:** Open Notifications and tap **Mark All Read**.

### Q: I'm not receiving push notifications. What should I check?
**A:** Make sure notifications are allowed for the Atar app in your phone's settings and that you're signed in. Even without push, your notifications are listed in the in-app Notifications screen.
