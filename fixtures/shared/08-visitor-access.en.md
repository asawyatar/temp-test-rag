# Visitor Access

### Q: How does visitor access work in the Atar app?
**A:** Residents register their visitors in advance. Each visit request generates a **QR check-in pass** that the resident shares with the guest. At the gate, the security staff or service professional scans the QR code to check the visitor in and later check them out. Every visit and its status is tracked in the **Visitors** tab.

### Q: How do I add a visitor?
**A:** Open the **Visitors** tab or tap **Add a Visitor** on the home screen, then fill in:
- **Unit**: the unit the guest is visiting.
- Visitor **First Name** and **Last Name**.
- **National ID** (10 digits, starting with 1, 2 or 3).
- **Phone Number**.
- **Nationality**.
- **Visit Date & Time**: must be in the future.
- **Visitor Has a Car?**: if yes, add the car's make/model, color and registration plate (letters and numbers).
- **Add additional visitor**: optional, for each extra person in the same visit.

Agree to the Terms & Conditions and submit.

### Q: What are the rules for a visitor's national ID?
**A:** It is required, must be exactly 10 characters, and must start with 1, 2 or 3 (the Saudi National ID / Iqama format).

### Q: Can I invite several people in one visit?
**A:** Yes. Use **Add additional visitor** to add each extra person. You can edit or remove them before submitting. They appear under **Additional Visitors** in the visit details.

### Q: Is there a limit on how many visitors I can invite?
**A:** Your community can set a visitor limit, for example a daily limit. If you exceed it, the app shows **Reached Your Limit** with the community's message, and the invite is not created.

### Q: How do I share the visitor pass with my guest?
**A:** Open the visit in the Visitors tab. The **Check-in Pass** shows a QR code ("Scan QR code to check in"). Tap **Share** to send it through WhatsApp, SMS or any other app. The guest shows the QR code at the gate.

### Q: When is the QR pass available?
**A:** The QR pass appears when the visit is **New** or **Approved**, and remains visible after check-in. If the visit is **Awaiting Approval**, the pass appears only once it is approved. Cancelled, rejected and checked-out visits have no usable pass.

### Q: What do the visitor statuses mean?
**A:**
- **New**: the visit was created.
- **Awaiting Approval**: the visit is waiting for approval by community management or security.
- **Approved**: the visit is approved and the pass is valid.
- **Checked-In**: the visitor has entered.
- **Checked-Out**: the visitor has left. The visit is complete.
- **Cancelled**: the visit was cancelled.
- **Rejected**: the visit was not approved.

### Q: Can I cancel a visit?
**A:** Yes, while it is New, Awaiting Approval or Approved. Open the visit, tap **Cancel**, and confirm **Cancel Visit**. Once a visitor has checked in, the visit can no longer be cancelled.

### Q: What information does a visit show after it ends?
**A:** The visit's status, visit date and time, check-in and check-out dates and times, who checked the visitor in or out, who cancelled or rejected it (if applicable), the unit, building and community, car details and additional visitors.

### Q: Where do I see past visits?
**A:** In the Visitors tab, the **History** tab lists past visits. **Active** lists upcoming and ongoing visits. You can search visitors by name.

### Q: How does security check a visitor in?
**A:** A service professional or security staff member opens **Visitors**, taps **Scan QR**, and points the camera at the guest's QR pass. Scanning starts automatically. They then tap **Check-In**. When the guest leaves they tap **Check-Out**, which closes the visit and moves it to history. If the code is not a visitor pass, the scanner says "This code isn't a visitor request." The camera permission must be allowed.

### Q: Can security cancel a visit?
**A:** Yes. The professional chooses a cancel reason (Incorrect visitor information, or Other) and adds a description.

### Q: Can I give a family member permanent access instead of inviting them each time?
**A:** Yes. Add them as an **Additional User** on your account (My Account → Additional Users). Additional users get access to property-related services and updates.
