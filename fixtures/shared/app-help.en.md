# Using the Resident App

This guide applies to every community that uses the resident app. Prices,
opening hours and rules are set by each community and are not covered here.

## Paying an invoice

Open the "Payments" tab to see your open invoices. Tap an invoice, choose
"Pay now" and pick a payment method. A receipt is saved under "Payment history"
as soon as the payment succeeds.

## Inviting a visitor

Open "Visitors" and tap "New invitation". Enter the visitor's name and the date
of the visit, then share the QR code with them from the confirmation screen.

## Adding a family member

Open "Profile", then "Family members", and tap "Add". Enter their name and
mobile number. They receive a text message with a link to activate their own
access to the app.

## Raising a service request

Open "Service requests" and tap "New request". Choose a category, describe the
problem and attach a photo if you have one.

## Changing the app language

Open "Profile", then "Settings", then "Language", and choose Arabic or English.
The app restarts to apply the change.
