# Service Requests

### Q: What kinds of services can I request in the Atar app?
**A:** Home and community services offered by your property management, such as plumbing, cleaning, air-conditioning, electrical work and car services. The exact list depends on your community. Open the **Services** tab and tap **Browse Services** to see everything available to you, grouped by category with the number of services in each.

### Q: How do I request a service?
**A:**
1. Open the **Services** tab (or tap **Request a Service** on the home screen).
2. Browse the categories and pick a service.
3. Fill in the request:
   - **Unit**: the unit the service is for.
   - **Description**: what you need.
   - **Attachments**: at least one photo of the issue.
   - **When should the service professional arrive?**: pick a date, then an available start time.
   - Car details (model, color, plate number) if the service is for your car.
4. Review the **Initial Assessment Fee**.
5. Accept the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy and Cancellation Policy.
6. Choose the payment method and tap **Book**.

You'll see "Your request has been created." and the request appears under **My Requests**.

### Q: How many photos can I attach to a service request?
**A:** At least one photo is required. You can attach up to 10 photos, each up to 30 MB. You can take a new photo with the camera or choose one from your library.

### Q: Why don't I see any available times?
**A:** The available times come from the service's schedule. On today's date, hours that have already passed are hidden. If a day shows "No times are available on this day", pick another date. Some services only work limited hours, and the app shows a note when that's the case.

### Q: What is the Initial Assessment Fee?
**A:** It covers the service professional's visit and their initial assessment. Any work beyond that is quoted separately, and you must approve the quote before that work starts. Some services are **Free**, and some show a starting price ("From …").

### Q: How do I pay for a service request?
**A:** The payment method offered at booking is **Cash**: you pay the service professional on site.

### Q: Some categories show a phone number or email instead of a request form. Why?
**A:** In some communities, requests for certain categories are handled by a call center. The app then shows the category's contact. Tap **Call** to phone them or copy the email address. If no contact is listed, contact your community office.

### Q: What are the statuses of a service request?
**A:**
- **New**: the request was created.
- **Assigned**: a service professional has been assigned.
- **Accepted**: the professional accepted the job.
- **In Progress**: the professional has started work.
- **Quote Raised**: the professional sent a quotation for extra work or parts.
- **Quote Accepted** / **Quote Rejected**: your decision on the quotation.
- **Completed**: the job is done.
- **Rejected**: the request was declined.
- **Cancelled**: the request was cancelled.

### Q: Where do I follow my requests?
**A:** In the Services tab, under **My Requests**. **Active** shows open requests. **History** shows requests that were completed or cancelled. Tap a request to see its details.

### Q: What does the request details page show?
**A:** The work progress (New → In-Progress → Completed), the unit, community and building, ticket ID, scheduled time, description and attachments, the assigned service professional (with a call button), initial-assessment and job-completion photos, any quotation, your confirmation code, and the **Request Log** (the history of status changes).

### Q: What is the confirmation code on my request?
**A:** It is a code that proves the job was completed. Share it with the service professional only **when the service is complete**. They enter it in their app to close the job.

### Q: How do I accept or reject a quotation?
**A:** When the status is "Quote Raised", open the request. The **Quotation** shows service fees, additional fees, spare parts and the total including 15% VAT. Tap **Accept Quotation** to approve the work or **Reject** to decline it.

### Q: Can I reschedule a service request?
**A:** Yes, while the request is still open. Open the request and choose **Reschedule Date & Time**, then pick a new date and an available time.

### Q: How do I cancel a service request?
**A:** Open the request and tap **Cancel Booking?**. Choose a reason and add a description, then confirm. You'll see "Your booking has been cancelled."

### Q: How do I rate a completed service?
**A:** After completion, open the request and tap **Rate**, or answer the rating pop-up the app shows when you sign in. Give 1 to 5 stars. If something could be better, pick what (Service Speed, Service Quality, Post-Service, Cleaning, Appearance or Other) and describe it. Each request can be rated once. After that the app shows your rating read-only.

### Q: I got a notification that my request was re-assigned. What does that mean?
**A:** Property management moved your request to a different service professional. The request continues as normal with the new professional.
