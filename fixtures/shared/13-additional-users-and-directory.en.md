# Additional Users and Directory

## Additional Users

### Q: What are additional users?
**A:** Additional users are family members or authorized occupants you add to your account. Adding them gives them access to property-related services and updates.

### Q: How do I add an additional user?
**A:** Open My Account → **Additional Users** → **Add An Additional User**, then fill in:
- **First Name** and **Last Name** (required; Arabic or Latin letters, up to two words).
- **Phone Number** (required).
- **Relationship** (required): Mother, Father, Wife, Son, Daughter, or Other.
- Optional: National ID / Residence Permit No., Email, Nationality, Gender and Date of Birth.

### Q: Can I edit or remove an additional user?
**A:** Yes. Tap the edit icon on the user to change their details. To remove them, open the user and tap **Delete User**, then confirm with **Yes**.

### Q: What relationships can I choose for an additional user?
**A:** Mother, Father, Wife, Son, Daughter, or Other.

## Directory

### Q: What is the Directory?
**A:** The Directory lists useful contacts for your community: contact details, service providers and community information published by management.

### Q: How do I use the Directory?
**A:** Open My Account → **Directory**. Search by name, tap an entry to see its entity name and phone number, and tap **Call** to phone them.

### Q: The Directory is empty. Why?
**A:** "No Directory Information Available" means management hasn't published any contacts yet.
