# Sign In and Account Access

### Q: How do I sign in to the Atar app?
**A:** Enter your **Business Username** (the username of your property-management company) and your **phone number**, then tap **Sign In**. The app sends a 4-digit code to your phone by SMS. Enter that code on the next screen and tap **Confirm**.

### Q: What is the "Business Username"?
**A:** It is the identifier of the property-management company that manages your community. It can contain letters, numbers and dashes only. If you don't know it, ask your community office.

### Q: Do I need a password to use the Atar app?
**A:** No. The Atar app has no password. You sign in with your phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS. After that you can turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint for quick sign-in.

### Q: How do I enter my phone number?
**A:** Pick your country code from the list (you can search by country name or code), then type the number. The app checks that the number is valid for the selected country and tells you if it is too short or too long.

### Q: How long is the verification code?
**A:** The code is 4 digits.

### Q: I didn't receive the verification code. What should I do?
**A:** It may take a few minutes for the code to arrive. When the countdown ("Resend code in …") finishes, tap **Resend code?** to get a new code. If the code has expired, the app tells you it is no longer valid and asks you to sign in again.

### Q: What happens if I enter the wrong code too many times?
**A:** The screen shows how many attempts you have left. If the code is incorrect or expired, the app shows "The code is incorrect or expired." When no attempts are left, go back to sign in and request a new code.

### Q: How do I turn on Face ID, Touch ID or fingerprint sign-in?
**A:** First sign in once with your business username and phone number. Then go to My Account → Account Settings and turn on **Enable Face ID** (or Touch ID, Fingerprint, or Iris Unlock, depending on your phone). Confirm with your biometric when asked. Next time, the sign-in screen offers a quick sign-in button.

### Q: Quick sign-in says "Quick sign-in isn't set up". Why?
**A:** Either biometrics are not set up on your phone, or you have not signed in with your business username and phone number on this device yet. Set up Face ID or fingerprint in your phone settings, sign in normally once, then enable quick sign-in in Account Settings.

### Q: Quick sign-in stopped working after I changed my phone's security settings. Why?
**A:** When you add or remove a fingerprint or face on your device, the phone invalidates stored biometric keys. The app shows "Quick sign-in needs setting up again". Sign in with your business username and phone number, then turn quick sign-in on again.

### Q: Face ID says "Too many attempts". What do I do?
**A:** Your phone has locked biometrics temporarily. Unlock the phone with its PIN or password, or sign in with your business username and phone number.

### Q: Why does the app say "Coming soon to mobile" after I sign in?
**A:** Your account has a role the mobile app does not support yet (for example an administrator account). Only owners, tenants and service professionals can use the mobile app. Please use the Atar web portal for now.

### Q: Why was I signed out with "Your session expired"?
**A:** Your sign-in session ended. Sign in again with your business username, phone number and a new verification code.

### Q: How do I sign out?
**A:** Go to My Account → Account Settings and tap **Log Out**.

### Q: What do I see the first time I open the app?
**A:** A short three-page introduction: "All your units in one place", "Request a service in seconds", and "Welcome guests and book facilities". You can tap **Skip** or **Continue**, then **Get Started**.
