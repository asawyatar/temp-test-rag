# Suggestions

### Q: What are suggestions in the Atar app?
**A:** Suggestions let you share ideas with your community team about what would make living there better. The team reads every suggestion.

### Q: How do I send a suggestion?
**A:** Tap **Send a Suggestion** on the home screen or open My Account → **Suggestions** → **Add Suggestion**. Fill in:
- **Unit** (required).
- **Title** (required).
- **Description** (required).
- **Attachments** (optional): images only, up to 30 MB each.

Then submit.

### Q: Can I attach files other than images to a suggestion?
**A:** No. Suggestions accept images only, up to 30 MB each.

### Q: Where do I see the suggestions I've sent?
**A:** My Account → **Suggestions** lists them. Tap one to see its ticket ID, sent date, community, building, unit, creation date, description and attachments.
