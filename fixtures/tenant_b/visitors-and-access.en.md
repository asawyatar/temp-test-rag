# Visitors and Access at Rimal Terrace

## Inviting visitors

Residents invite visitors from the resident app. The visitor receives a QR code
to show at the gate. The code is valid for 12 hours from the time it is issued.
Each unit may invite up to 10 visitors per day.

## Overnight guests

A guest may stay overnight for up to 3 nights in a row once registered in the
app. Longer stays need approval from the community management office.

## Deliveries and contractors

Delivery drivers enter through the North Entrance and must leave within 20
minutes. Outside contractors must be registered at least two days before
their visit.

## Entrances

The Main Entrance is for residents and is open 24 hours. The North Entrance is
for visitors and deliveries and is open from 07:00 to 23:00.
