# Fees and Payments at Rimal Terrace

## Annual service fee

The annual service fee is 60 SAR per square metre of the unit. It is billed in
two instalments each year, on 1 January and 1 July. The fee covers security,
cleaning of shared areas, landscaping and lift maintenance.

## Late payment

Each invoice is due within 30 days of its issue date. A late fee of 2% of the
invoice amount is added to invoices unpaid after 30 days.

## Ways to pay

Invoices can be paid in the resident app by mada card, credit card or bank
transfer. Cash and cheques are accepted at the management office on working
days between 09:00 and 15:00.

## Refunds

Overpayments are credited to the next invoice by default. To receive the money
back instead, contact the management office; refunds take up to 20 working days.
